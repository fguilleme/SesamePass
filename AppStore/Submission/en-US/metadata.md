# SesamePass — English (U.S.)

## Subtitle

Passports and IDs via NFC

## Promotional text

Read the NFC chip in your passport or identity card, keep it available offline, and export a PDF copy or encrypted archive.

## Description

SesamePass lets you read and keep information from compatible NFC travel documents on your iPhone.

Photograph the machine-readable zone (MRZ), then hold your iPhone against the document. SesamePass establishes a secure connection with the chip and retrieves the available information, including identity details, document data, and the photograph.

Features:

• Read compatible biometric passports
• Read compatible TD1 electronic identity cards
• Scan the MRZ with the camera
• Review cryptographic checks for the data read
• Access documents offline
• Use local storage protected by iOS
• Enable optional encrypted iCloud Backup
• Export a readable PDF or encrypted archive
• Delete a document locally or from both the iPhone and iCloud

Your data remains under your control. SesamePass uses no third-party servers, analytics, advertising tracking, or ads. Data sent to iCloud is encrypted before upload.

SesamePass is not affiliated with any government authority. Information and PDFs produced by the app are personal copies and never replace an official passport, identity card, or travel document.

NFC reading requires a compatible iPhone and supported electronic document. Some cards that require a CAN, PIN, or unsupported protocol variant may not be readable.
